IHateRecruiters.
An experiment in professional networking

It's a social network where we removed social.

What if professionals could be discovered for their work, without having to perform being employable?

THE IDEA

Peers, not recruiters.
Reputation, not performance.

Professional networks are great at making people searchable. They're less great at letting people simply be professionals.

IHateRecruiters explores a quieter alternative: useful connections, real opportunities, and fewer incentives for personal-brand theatre.

Not another feed.

Less self-promotion. More signal. Built around the people doing the work.

Early concept. Not yet an active service.
