THE RULES

What makes this different?

01
Post the job. Not the pitch.

Share a link to a real job opening. No sales copy, inspirational stories or employer-branding speeches.

02
Questions first. Answers second.

Ask what the job is really like. The person who shared it can answer, but can't start the conversation.

03
No personal brand required.

Use a pseudonym. No CV, profile picture or carefully curated career story.

You won't need an account just to browse. When participation opens, you'll be able to create a pseudonymous account to share jobs and join discussions.

It's a social network where we removed social.

Less self-promotion. More signal. Built around the people doing the work.

Early concept. Job browsing and accounts aren't live yet.
